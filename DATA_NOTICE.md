# Third-party data notice

This repository contains derived analytical artifacts produced from third-party market and macroeconomic data.

The Apache-2.0 license applies only to original HilmarCorp code, tests, automation and documentation. It does not grant rights to third-party market data.

Raw provider caches are intentionally excluded from the public repository.

The canonical research pipeline references data from Binance, Yahoo Finance, FRED, the Federal Reserve Bank of Chicago, the US Bureau of Labor Statistics and exchange-calendar metadata supplied by `exchange_calendars`.

Use, redistribution and downstream storage of provider data remain subject to the applicable provider terms and rights.

The committed `outputs/digital_gold_v4_1_1/` package is a derived research snapshot. Exact upstream-source reconstruction may change if providers revise historical records, adjusted prices, interfaces or metadata.

See `DATA_PROVENANCE.md` and `source_registry.json` for source-specific treatment and limitations.
