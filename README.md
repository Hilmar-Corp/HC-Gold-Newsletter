# HC-Gold-Newsletter

Research repository supporting HilmarCorp's analysis:

**Is “digital gold” a good comparison?**

A quantitative comparison of Bitcoin and gold across macro-financial environments, including equity stress, real rates, the US dollar, credit conditions, inflation and risk aversion.

## Research status

Frozen research version: **V4.1.1**

Primary sample: **2017-08-17 → 2026-08-26**

The research pipeline includes:
- synchronized Bitcoin / GLD market closes;
- macro-factor regressions;
- HAC / Newey-West inference;
- joint Wald tests;
- conditional distributions;
- independent stress episodes;
- rolling sensitivities;
- leave-one-year-out robustness;
- CPI publication-date analysis.

This repository contains research material and does not constitute investment advice.
